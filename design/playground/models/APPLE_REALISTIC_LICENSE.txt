Realistic apple meshes for the material playground.

apple_realistic.glb
  Exterior geometry is Poly Haven "Food Apple 01" by Oliver Harries.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/food_apple_01
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
  https://polyhaven.com/license
  Textures are not included. The stalk keeps the scanned, irregular
  cross-section. It is not a generated cylinder.
