apple_whole_real.glb and apple_half_real.glb are derived from that scan.
The SQ mesh is Loop-subdivided once. Color and normal JPEGs are embedded;
texels just outside the UV islands are dilated so the orange atlas background
does not bleed. The half keeps the scanned skin on one side of a vertical cut,
with an authored concave flesh, core and two seeds.
